# Farm archive requirements

Players use the archive to go from a tutorial or design number (DN) to the files for that exact build.

- Preserve direct links such as `/dn/539`. Treat a Bedrock prefix as part of the identifier, so Java and Bedrock designs with the same digits remain distinct.
- Show the edition and farm type alongside the tutorial and world download. Display schematic and material actions when the underlying assets are available.
- Keep the catalog usable on a phone. A player arriving from YouTube should be able to open the matching world without interacting with the 3D viewer.

## Acceptance example

Look up DN 539, open its detail page and follow the video and world actions. The record should identify an iron farm and both destinations should match its archive entry.
